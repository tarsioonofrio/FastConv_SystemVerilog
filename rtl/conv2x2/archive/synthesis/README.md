# Archived 2x2 synthesis configurations

These configurations and their reports were moved out of `synthesis/` to keep
the active 2x2 synthesis set limited to 16-bit configurations. Their files
and results are preserved for historical reference; no result was deleted.

The active configurations remain under `rtl/conv2x2/synthesis/` and are the
two `conv-i24-...-nbits16` column configurations.
