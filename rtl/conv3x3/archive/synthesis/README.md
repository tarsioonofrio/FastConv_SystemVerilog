# Archived 3x3 synthesis configurations

These configurations and their reports were moved out of `synthesis/` to keep
the active 3x3 synthesis set limited to 16-bit configurations. Their files
and results are preserved for historical reference; no result was deleted.

The active configurations remain under `rtl/conv3x3/synthesis/` and are the
IFN9 and TCN9 column configurations with the `-nbits16` suffix.
