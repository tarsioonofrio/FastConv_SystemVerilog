# ASIC flow status — IFN9 m18

The flow ran on Paxos with Genus/Joules 21.12-s068_1 and Xcelium 23.03-s003,
using the mapped netlist, nominal SDF, canonical workload, and 2 ns testbench
clock. The core and dataset match the local files by SHA-256; the synthesis
source checkout was commit `f7c32122eb67f41ef8bd90a6d427ef8f74ab0616`.

| Stage | Result |

| SDF path-delay annotation | 253,611 / 253,611 (100%) |

| Joules power analysis | Completed from the SHM generated by the passing run; typical corner, 0.90 V / 25 C, PLE |

The initial nine corner mismatches came from the testbench reacting to a narrow
43 ps `p_end` pulse while the output FSM was in `READ_OUTPUT` and
`p_output_wr=0`. The testbench now recognizes completion only on a clock edge
where `p_end`, `p_output_en`, and `p_output_wr` are all high. With that
synchronous final-write handshake, the job completed in 14,876 ns (7,438
measured cycles) and passed the final output golden check.

The typical-corner report gives leakage of 0.129178 mW, internal power of
5.27456 mW, switching power of 3.69429 mW, and total power of 9.09803 mW.
Thus dynamic power (internal + switching) is 8.96885 mW. Category totals are
2.66168 mW register, 6.25812 mW logic, and 0.178233 mW clock.

The Joules script imports the SHM starting at 0 ns, so the reported average
covers the full simulation activity (including reset/startup), not a separately
windowed active-job-only interval. Timing delays were fully annotated, but only
17.85% of SDF timing checks matched the available cell models; retain this
limitation when comparing power results.

The final Xcelium/Joules logs and run hashes are in
`sim/diagnostics/power_final_20261005/`; canonical logs are in `sim/` and the
