# WPN16 FPGA campaign: corrected 8, 16, and 32 MACs at 16 bits

## Result

The inverse-row accumulation decode was regenerated with statically decoded
row-batch cases. On the ZCU104 (`xczu7ev-ffvc1156-2-e`), the 8-, 16-, and
32-MAC designs all pass the post-route functional golden test. The 8- and
16-MAC implementations meet the 317 MHz timing constraint. The 32-MAC default
implementation misses timing by 9 ps at one endpoint, despite passing the
golden test.

The P3F power values below are post-route functional-activity estimates from
the reported implementation. For m32, they do not establish a realizable
timing-closed 317 MHz operating point. Do not use m32 throughput or energy as
if 317 MHz had passed timing.

## Power reports

top-level captures ran the same canonical 16-bit truncated WPN16 workload.
All three simulations reported 2,304 write beats, 9,216 physical output

## Timing and the rejected Explore run

The standard m32 critical path starts at
`r_hadamard_product_reg_reg[2][3]` and ends at
`r_output_write_reg[1][17]`. It crosses the inverse/accumulation logic and six
`CARRY8` stages; the routed path delay is 3.206 ns, with 1.444 ns of logic and
1.762 ns of routing. This is the remaining 317 MHz timing issue.

A separate `Explore` + post-route `phys_opt_design -directive Explore` run
improved WNS to -0.001 ns, still one endpoint short of timing closure. More
importantly, its post-route functional simulation failed all 2,700 golden
outputs. That run is diagnostic only: its SAIF/power is not accepted and the
standard implementation remains the functional reference.
