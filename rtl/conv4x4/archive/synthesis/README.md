# Archived 4x4 ASIC synthesis configurations

These directories contain preserved synthesis campaigns that are not part of
the currently selected 4x4 RTL set. They were moved from `rtl/conv4x4/synthesis/`
without deleting their reports, scripts, logs, or other campaign artifacts.

The active synthesis directory retains the i60/prefetch24 WPN16 column RTL at
8, 16, and 32 multipliers, in both 20-bit and 16-bit runs, plus the selected
TCN16 `frac6` variants at 6, 12, and 18 multipliers. The archived TCN16
reports refer to older non-`frac6` source variants and must not be presented
as results for the selected `frac6` RTLs.
